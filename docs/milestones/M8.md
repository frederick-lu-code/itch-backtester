## Questions a reviewer might ask

- **What is the weakest part of the project?** The benchmarks. The engineering around them is
  sound (generated tables, guarded runs, deleted bad data), but the numbers come from a laptop
  on battery, there are no hardware counters or flame graphs, and three sections are empty.
- **What would you check first if you inherited it?** Run the suite on a Linux x86-64 machine.
  The Linux paths of three scripts, and the x86 branch of the tick counter, have never run
  outside CI's compile step.
- **What is the strongest part?** The correctness evidence: an independent Python book that
  agrees level for level over a whole real day, PnL recomputed independently and equal to the
  last price unit, byte-identical output across repeat runs, recorder modes, book coverage and
  shard counts, and closing prices that reconcile with public data.
- **How much of this did a person review?** The plan for the first two milestones. The rest
  was built without sign-off at the owner's instruction, which is why the decisions log and
  the debugging log are as detailed as they are.
