## Questions a reviewer might ask

- **Why does the writer use shifts when the parser uses memcpy plus a byte swap?** Shifts are
  endian-independent and speed is irrelevant in test code. Using a different technique from the
  parser also means the two cannot share a byte-order mistake.
- **How do you know the writer's layouts are right?** Each was checked against the official
  specification, the writer throws if a message's length differs from the specified one, and
  the tests state every offset independently of the writer.
- **Why is the first fuzz target empty?** It proved the preset and the CI step worked before
  there was a parser to fuzz. M1 replaced it.
